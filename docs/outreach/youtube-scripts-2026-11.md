# YouTube scripts: November 2026

Issue #16, action 9 in the [AI visibility research](../research/2026-11-04-ai-visibility.md). YouTube is one of the domains AI Overviews cite most often. Short social clips rarely get cited. Longer videos whose titles match what instructors search for can be.

John records them; Claude embeds each one on its matching page once the URL exists. The task is in ✅ John's to-do ("Record 2–3 YouTube walkthrough videos").

## Rules for all three

- **Demo school only:** one fictional school, plausible UK names, no real pupil data. If a card payment appears, it's in Stripe test mode.
- **Show only what is live today.** Text and WhatsApp reminders, recurring lesson series, test bookings and results, bulk import and parent accounts are **not** live. Don't show them or say "coming soon" on camera; the video will outlive the roadmap.
- **Prices:** say only "free until 31 March 2027, no card needed". Don't quote the 2027 plan prices on camera: they can change, and a video can't be edited.
- **Length and format:** 3–6 minutes, screen recording plus your voice. Face-cam is optional. Use 1080p or higher, at the same zoom throughout.
- **Upload:** use the title, description and chapters below exactly; they match the searches. Turn on auto-captions and correct any wrong words (DVSA, ADI, DriveSchoolPro). Add the video to a "DriveSchoolPro walkthroughs" playlist.
- **Description links** use `?utm_source=youtube&utm_medium=video&utm_campaign=<slug>` so signups are attributed.
