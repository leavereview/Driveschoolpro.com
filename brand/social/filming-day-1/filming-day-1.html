The one rule in the carNobody holds or touches a phone while the car is moving. Every phone scene is parked, engine off (engine on only for the lights and washers shots, still parked). Moving shots are filmed by your friend from the pavement.

Before Saturday

  • Watch the 5 short tutorials at the bottom of this page (about 45 minutes in total). The first two matter most.
  • Read through Block 2 out loud once. You don't need to learn it: on the day you'll read each line from the prompter, look at the lens and say it.
  • Put the phone screens on both phones: on the Mac, the folder "DSP phone screens" is on your Desktop. AirDrop it to both phones and save to Photos (in Photos, tap the image once to hide the buttons). Or open this page on the prop phone and tap a screen: it opens full screen on black.
  • Choose your clothes: one plain shirt or jumper for the desk pieces, no stripes or logos. Your daughter: something she'd wear to a lesson.
  • Clear 20 GB of space on the filming phone. Charge everything.

Camera settings (iPhone, once)

  • Settings → Camera → Record Video → 4K at 30 fps.
  • Settings → Camera → Formats → Most Compatible (easier to edit).
  • Settings → Camera → Grid on. Keep eyes on the top third line.
  • Always film upright (vertical), with the back camera, 1× lens. Wipe the lens first.
  • Before each shot, press and hold on your face (or the phone screen) until "AE/AF LOCK" appears, so focus and brightness don't wander.
  • Count 2 seconds before you start and 2 seconds after you finish. Do every shot twice.

Android: same idea. 4K 30 fps (or 1080p if 4K isn't offered), grid on, film upright, tap and hold to lock focus.

Phones in the shot

  • Do Not Disturb on both prop phones, brightness to full, Auto-Lock to Never.
  • Only the demo screens on show. No real pupils, messages or numbers.
  • Hold the phone still for 3 seconds on each screen so it can be read.

Kit list

  • Filming phone, tripod, your clip-on mic (test it: record 10 seconds and play it back with headphones)
  • Your phone and your daughter's phone, both with the screens loaded
  • A laptop or iPad for the prompter (this page)
  • Tuition car: clean inside and out, L plates, roof sign if you have one
  • Props: paper diary, a pile of receipts, calculator, a mug, a few £10 and £20 notes, a coat, ice scraper, screenwash
  • Water and snacks. Breaks matter more than you think.

After filming

Five tips that matter most

  • Light in front of you, never behind. Face a window.
  • Phone at eye level, lens at your eyes. Look into the lens, not at the screen.
  • Mic clipped mid-chest, a hand's width below your chin, cable hidden under your shirt.
  • Say one line, stop, breathe, next line. Claude cuts the gaps out.
  • Mistake? Pause, then say the whole line again. Never stop the recording to start over.
