- **Date / time:** Monday 2 November, 07:15
- **Channels:** Both

- **Hook:** The clocks went back last weekend. For some of your pupils, this week is their first lesson in the dark.

**Caption**
> The clocks went back on 25 October. For some of your pupils, this week is their first lesson in the dark.
>
> A few things that help the first one go well:
>
> 1. Tell them in advance. "Tonight we'll be driving in the dark" lets them come ready, not anxious.
> 2. Start on a route they already know. New roads and darkness at once is too much.
> 3. Do a lights check together before moving off: dipped beam, main beam and the warning light, rear lights.
> 4. Give their eyes a couple of minutes to adjust, and talk about not staring at oncoming headlights.
> 5. Point out pedestrians and cyclists early. They're much harder to spot after dark.
> 6. Leave time at the end to talk about how it felt.
>
> Driving in the dark is DVSA skill 24. Our guide has more.

### N05 · Tip: headlight rules learners get wrong
- **Date / time:** Wednesday 4 November, 07:15
- **Channels:** Both
- **Format:** Carousel, 7 slides
- **Hook:** Five Highway Code headlight rules worth going over before your next evening lesson.

**Caption**
> Five Highway Code headlight rules worth going over before your next evening lesson.
>
> 1. Use headlights at night (half an hour after sunset to half an hour before sunrise), except on roads with lit street lighting. (Rule 113)
> 2. Sidelights and rear number plate lights must be on between sunset and sunrise. (Rule 113)
> 3. Don't dazzle. In a stopped queue, once the car behind has stopped, take your foot off the brake so your brake lights don't glare. (Rule 114)
> 4. Keep dipped when overtaking until you're level with the other vehicle. (Rule 115)
> 5. Dazzled by oncoming lights? Slow down, and stop if you need to. (Rule 115)
>
> Test prep: switching on dipped headlights ("show me") and main beam ("tell me") are both on the official list.
